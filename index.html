HOW TO PLAY

Goal

Race through the neon city, dodge traffic and barriers, grab energy orbs and beat your best score.

Desktop controls

  • A / ← move left   D / → move right
  • W / ↑ / Space hold to boost
  • Esc / P pause or resume

Mobile controls

Tap ◀ and ▶ (or swipe left/right), hold BOOST, tap ❚❚ to pause.

Tips

  • Orbs give points, credits and a little boost. Chain them for a combo multiplier (up to x5).
  • Green shield pickups absorb one crash (7 seconds).
  • Boost is faster and scores more, but leaves less time to react.
  • Spend credits in the Garage on faster cars.
